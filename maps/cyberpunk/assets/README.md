# Cyberpunk artwork

The nine `cyberpunk-sign-*` SVGs are original Tileflow vector drawings, released under the
repository’s Apache-2.0 license. A dark chamfered plate frames transit, medical, lodging, culture,
food, retail, nature, landmark and generic-place pictograms. Their inks match the map’s
destination (yellow), activity (magenta) and nature (jade) families. No game logos or artwork
were copied. Shape distinguishes categories independently of colour.

The crosswalk adapts Tileflow’s original Streets artwork. The ground data grid retains its
original Tileflow provenance. Category signs replace the former target brackets. Font licensing
is in [fonts/README.md](fonts/README.md).
